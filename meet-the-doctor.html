Meet Dr. Vecchioni.

Dr. William D. Vecchioni has served the Dearborn Heights community for 40 years and is a District 7 alumnus. A graduate of Sherman College of Chiropractic in South Carolina, he earned a post-graduate fellowship in Applied Spinal Biomechanics for personalized spinal analysis and rehabilitative exercises for spinal reconstruction.

He is proficient in five chiropractic techniques and uses the Pulstar FRAS® — a computer-assisted adjusting instrument gentle enough for infants to centenarians.
