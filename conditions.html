Conditions we care for

Topics to discuss with
a qualified clinician.

Dr. Vecchioni has experience evaluating each of the conditions below. An individual assessment is needed to determine what care is appropriate. These descriptions do not promise treatment outcomes.

Neck pain

Stiffness, restricted motion, post-injury soreness, and chronic tightness.

Mid back pain

Between-the-shoulders aching, postural strain, and rib-cage discomfort.

Low back pain

Sciatica, disc problems, muscle spasm — everyday strains and acute injuries.

Headaches

Tension-type and cervicogenic headaches with cervical involvement.

Sinus & allergies

Upper cervical technique and sinus drainage considerations.

Fatigue

Generalized tiredness without a clear medical cause.

Mood swings

Nervous-system considerations alongside appropriate referrals.

Sleep disturbances

Difficulty falling asleep, waking unrefreshed, and night restlessness.

Auto injuries

Whiplash and soft-tissue recovery following a motor vehicle accident.

Sports injuries

Sprains, strains, and overuse injuries for athletes of every level.

Results vary by individual. Chiropractic care is not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment. The doctor will refer when appropriate.
