Techniques & instruments

Multiple techniques.
An individual approach.

Explore the techniques and instrument listed by the practice. The choice of approach depends on your health history and assessment.

Pierce Stillwagon

Pierce Stillwagon Technique

A classic technique focused on the upper cervical spine — with an emphasis on spinal assessment.

Suitability depends on an individual assessment. Ask about benefits, risks and alternatives.

Thompson

Thompson Technique

A drop-table approach that uses the table's segmented surface to assist the adjustment.

Suitability depends on an individual assessment. Ask about benefits, risks and alternatives.

HIO

Hole-In-One (HIO)

A targeted upper cervical adjustment designed to address atlas and axis misalignment.

Suitability depends on an individual assessment. Ask about benefits, risks and alternatives.

Diversified

Diversified Technique

The most widely-taught hands-on adjusting method — quick, controlled thrusts intended to support joint mobility.

Suitability depends on an individual assessment. Ask about benefits, risks and alternatives.

Pulstar FRAS

Pulstar FRAS® Instrument

A computer-assisted adjusting instrument that delivers gentle, repeatable impulses as one option for instrument-assisted care.

Suitability depends on an individual assessment. Ask about benefits, risks and alternatives.

Applied Spinal Biomechanics

A.S.B. Fellowship

Post-graduate specialization in personalized spinal analysis and rehabilitative exercises for spinal reconstruction.

Suitability depends on an individual assessment. Ask about benefits, risks and alternatives.
