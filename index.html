Techniques & instruments

Multiple techniques.
One precise result.

Dr. Vecchioni is proficient in five chiropractic techniques and uses a computer-assisted adjusting instrument gentle enough for the entire family.

Pierce Stillwagon

Pierce Stillwagon Technique

A classic technique focused on the upper cervical spine — where many whole-body issues originate.

Used when: headaches, migraines, neck pain, sinus complaints.

Thompson

Thompson Technique

A drop-table approach that uses the table's segmented surface to make adjustments precise and gentle.

Used when: full-spine correction, postural issues, disc problems.

HIO

Hole-In-One (HIO)

A targeted upper cervical adjustment designed to address atlas and axis misalignment.

Used when: chronic headaches, neurological symptoms, brain-body communication.

Diversified

Diversified Technique

The most widely-taught hands-on adjusting method — quick, controlled thrusts to restore joint motion.

Used when: most musculoskeletal complaints and joint restrictions.

Pulstar FRAS

Pulstar FRAS® Instrument

A computer-assisted adjusting instrument that delivers gentle, repeatable impulses — gentle enough for infants to centenarians.

Used when: patients who prefer low-force care, pediatric and senior patients.

Applied Spinal Biomechanics

A.S.B. Fellowship

Post-graduate specialization in personalized spinal analysis and rehabilitative exercises for spinal reconstruction.

Used when: long-term correction, posture restoration, structural rehabilitation.

What we care for

Topics to discuss with
a qualified clinician.

An individual assessment is needed to determine what care is appropriate. These descriptions do not promise treatment outcomes — only that Dr. Vecchioni has experience evaluating each.

Neck pain

Stiffness, restricted motion, post-injury soreness, and chronic tightness.

Mid back pain

Between-the-shoulders aching, postural strain, and rib-cage discomfort.

Low back pain

Sciatica, disc problems, muscle spasm — everyday strains and acute injuries.

Headaches

Tension-type and cervicogenic headaches with cervical involvement.

Sinus & allergies

Upper cervical technique and sinus drainage considerations.

Fatigue

Generalized tiredness without a clear medical cause.

Mood swings

Nervous-system considerations alongside appropriate referrals.

Sleep disturbances

Difficulty falling asleep, waking unrefreshed, and night restlessness.

Auto injuries

Whiplash and soft-tissue recovery following a motor vehicle accident.

Sports injuries

Sprains, strains, and overuse injuries for athletes of every level.
