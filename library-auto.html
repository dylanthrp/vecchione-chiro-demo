Injury recovery

Auto & sports injuries.

Whiplash, soft-tissue strains, and return-to-activity timelines after an accident.

What whiplash is

Whiplash is a soft-tissue injury to the neck caused by a rapid back-and-forth motion — most commonly from a rear-end auto collision. Symptoms may appear immediately or take several days to develop.

What sports injuries look like

Sprains, strains, and overuse injuries are common in athletics of every level. The pattern of the injury — what happened, what hurts now, what limits your motion — guides the evaluation.

  • How the injury happened
  • Where the pain is and what makes it worse
  • Which movements are restricted
  • Whether imaging or a referral is needed

What the doctor evaluates

Dr. Vecchioni examines the affected area and the joints above and below to determine the extent of the injury and the compensations it has created. He then explains which technique fits your situation.

What conservative care looks like

If the issue is mechanical, the doctor uses the appropriate technique — Diversified, Thompson, or Pulstar FRAS — and may recommend rehabilitative exercise for a clear return-to-activity timeline.

When to call the office

Call (313) 277-1100 if your symptoms have lasted more than a few days, are getting worse, or are limiting your daily activities. The first call is for you to find out whether care makes sense for your situation.

What this article is not

This is patient education, not medical advice. It is intended to help you have a better conversation with a qualified clinician. It does not promise any specific outcome.
