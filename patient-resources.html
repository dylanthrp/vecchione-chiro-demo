Before your first visit

Bring identification

Before you visit

A few practical questions.

Start here, then confirm the details with the office.

How do I arrange an appointment?

Call (313) 277-1100 to ask about appointments. This preview cannot book a visit, accept appointment requests or confirm availability.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Ask the office about its requirements. It may be useful to bring identification, a medication list and relevant records or imaging. Bring questions about your symptoms and goals; do not upload records to this preview.

Are weekend appointments available?

Weekend hours are not listed on the clinic's website. Call the office to ask about availability rather than assuming a Saturday appointment is available. Published hours are shown in Eastern time.

What will care cost? Is insurance accepted?

The clinic's official site mentions affordable wellness plans. Ask the office for current fees, accepted coverage and any out-of-pocket costs. This preview does not verify insurance or guarantee coverage.

Can I send a health question through this website?

No. There are no patient forms, chat or messaging services here. Contact the clinic directly for its approved communication options. For a medical emergency, call 911 rather than waiting for an office response.
