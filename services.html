Services & techniques

Five techniques.
One precise result.

Dr. Vecchioni is proficient in five chiropractic techniques and a post-graduate fellow in Applied Spinal Biomechanics. Here is what each one does and when he uses it.

Pierce Stillwagon

Pierce Stillwagon Technique

A classic technique focused on the upper cervical spine — the atlas and axis — where many whole-body issues originate.

Used for: headaches, migraines, neck pain, sinus complaints.

Thompson

Thompson Technique

A drop-table approach that uses the table's segmented surface to make adjustments precise, light, and effective.

Used for: full-spine correction, postural issues, disc problems.

HIO

Hole-In-One (HIO) Technique

A targeted upper-cervical adjustment designed to address atlas and axis misalignment.

Used for: chronic headaches, neurological symptoms, brain-body communication.

Diversified

Diversified Technique

The most widely-taught hands-on adjusting method — quick, controlled thrusts to restore joint motion.

Used for: most musculoskeletal complaints and joint restrictions.

Pulstar FRAS®

Pulstar FRAS® Instrument

A computer-assisted adjusting instrument that delivers gentle, repeatable impulses — gentle enough for infants to centenarians.

Used for: patients who prefer low-force care, pediatric and senior patients.

Fellowship

Applied Spinal Biomechanics

Post-graduate specialization in personalized spinal analysis and rehabilitative exercises for spinal reconstruction.

Used for: long-term correction, posture restoration, structural rehabilitation.
