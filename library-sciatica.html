Nerve

Sciatica.

How the sciatic nerve works, why pain radiates, and how a chiropractor evaluates it.

How the sciatic nerve works

The sciatic nerve is the longest nerve in the body — it runs from the lower lumbar spine through the buttocks and down the back of each leg. Pain that radiates along this path is usually called sciatica, even when the cause varies.

What the doctor evaluates

Dr. Vecchioni examines the lumbar spine and lower extremity to determine where the nerve irritation is coming from — disc, joint, or muscle — and whether a referral is needed.

  • Where the pain radiates and what makes it worse
  • Whether there is numbness, tingling, or weakness
  • Range of motion in the lumbar spine
  • Whether imaging or a referral is needed

What conservative care looks like

Care depends on the cause of your symptoms, your medical history and an examination. A qualified clinician can discuss options, expected benefits, risks and alternatives, including whether referral is appropriate. No particular technique or outcome can be recommended from this article.

When to seek help

Do not wait for an office appointment for sudden severe headache, new weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, chest pain, or serious injury. Seek urgent medical care; call 911 for an emergency.

Call (313) 277-1100 if your symptoms have lasted more than a few days, are getting worse, or are limiting your daily activities. The first call is for you to find out whether care makes sense for your situation.
